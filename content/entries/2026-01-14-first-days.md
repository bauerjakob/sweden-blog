The plane landed in the dark and it stayed dark. I keep having to remind
myself it was only four in the afternoon.

My room is small and very warm, which turns out to be the whole trick of this
country — it's brutal outside and then you step through a door and it's
candles and wool and someone offering you coffee. I think I'm going to be fine
here. I think I'm going to like it a lot.
