The course is taught in English, which everyone here says the way you'd
apologise for something. It is, in fact, better English than mine.

What nobody warned me about is the silence. A lecturer asks a question and
there's this long, calm pause where at home someone would already be talking.
Here they just… think about it. The first few times I nearly filled the gap
myself out of sheer panic. Now I'm learning to sit in it.

Also: coffee breaks are not optional. There is a *word* for it — **fika** —
and it is apparently a small betrayal to skip one. I can work with a culture
like this.
