You can get to actual islands on the same card you use for the tram, so a few
of us did, on a whim, on a Sunday. Twenty minutes of open water and the city
just lets go of you.

We had no plan. We walked the length of one island, ate sandwiches on a rock
out of the wind, and got quietly, thoroughly cold. It was one of the best days
I've had here.
