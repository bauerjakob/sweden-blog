There is a street of old wooden houses called Haga where the whole trade
appears to be enormous cinnamon buns, and I have decided to take it personally,
one bun at a time. Cardamom in the dough. Pearl sugar on top. Roughly the
diameter of a dinner plate.

Grandma, this is the kind you'd have made me guess the recipe for.
