A friend took me to her family's place on the coast for Midsummer, which is the
one everybody had been promising me since January — *wait until Midsummer.*

They were right. It doesn't get dark. Not properly, not at all. We ate pickled
everything and new potatoes and strawberries, someone had a guitar, small kids
made flower crowns and then wore them into the sea, and at what turned out to
be midnight the sky was still the colour of the inside of a shell.

Six hours of daylight in January and now this — a day that simply forgets to
end. I keep thinking about how the same place holds both, and how I got to be
here for the whole swing of it.
